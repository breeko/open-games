About this test level & tools

Original campaign reference prototype: Southport, The Farmlands, Pagras, Twin Rivers Pass, Silveroak Forest and The Citadel; four tower families, map-specific waves and upgrades, tower skills and power star upgrades, no hero. Combat and animation are still being compared with the original.

The artwork comes from community exports of Ironhide’s game. This is an unofficial reference prototype. Sources & credits.

Towers & powers

Battle pauses while you read. Times are at 1× speed. Ranges and blast radii use fixed battlefield units, not screen pixels; range rings show their size on the map. Damage is before armor and resistance.

Tower skills cost battle gold. Train powers with campaign stars under ★ Power upgrades. Heroes, tower star-upgrade branches and later-map tower types are not included.
